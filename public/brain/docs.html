Quick start

Make a sandbox key in the console, under Keys. Keys go on your servers only, never in a web page or an app.

Base address: https://talihq.com.au/brain/api/v1. Send your key as a Bearer token. Everything is JSON.

1. Tell Tali what happened

curl https://talihq.com.au/brain/api/v1/events \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer thb_test_..." -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "business_id": "biz-1", "business_name": "Coastline Realty",
    "client_id": "client-1", "client_name": "Priya Shah",
    "type": "note",
    "text": "Loved the light at Bower St, hated the road noise. Budget up to $1.25m."
  }'

Tali makes a brain for biz-1 the first time she sees it, and keeps the facts that will still matter in a month.

2. Ask for an answer

curl https://talihq.com.au/brain/api/v1/answers \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer thb_test_..." -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "business_id": "biz-1", "client_id": "client-1",
    "kind": "draft",
    "message": "Can we see Lauderdale Ave this Saturday? What are the strata fees?",
    "context": "22 Lauderdale Ave: quiet street, strata $1,150 a quarter, open Saturday 10:30am"
  }'
{
  "id": "70015d2c-...",
  "kind": "draft",
  "text": "Hi Priya, Saturday at 10:30 works for 22 Lauderdale Ave ...",
  "used": [{ "id": "91e7...", "fact": "Wants to be able to walk to the beach. Dislikes road noise." }],
  "noticed": [],
  "booking": null,
  "actions": [],
  "answers_charged": 1
}

3. Tell Tali what the person did with it

curl -X POST https://talihq.com.au/brain/api/v1/answers/70015d2c-.../outcome \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer thb_test_..." -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "outcome": "approved" }'

Outcomes are approved, edited or rejected. They power the approval numbers in your console.

The ideas

  • Business. One of your customers. You send your own ID for it as business_id. Each business has its own private brain.
  • Client. A person that business looks after. You send your own ID as client_id.
  • Memory. One short fact Tali keeps, about a client or about the business itself. A memory is either kept, or suggested when Tali noticed it herself and a person has not said yes yet.
  • Event. Something that happened: a note, a message, a booking. Sending events is how Tali learns. It is free.
  • Answer. Something Tali writes for a person to approve. Each one counts as 1 answer on your plan.
  • Sandbox and live. Test keys (thb_test_) work on separate sandbox brains and never bill. Live keys (thb_live_) come with a plan.

Tali never sends, books or spends. She returns drafts and suggestions. Your software acts after your user says yes.

Events

POST/v1/events

Send one event, or up to 20 in events. Fields on the request: business_id (needed), business_name, client_id, client_name, learn (send false to store without learning).

Fields on each event: type (note, message, email, call, booking, form, other), text, direction (in from the client, out to the client, or leave it out for internal), at (when it happened), and id (your own ID for it, so the same event sent twice is only stored once).

{
  "business_id": "biz-1", "client_id": "client-1",
  "events": [
    { "id": "email-881", "type": "email", "direction": "in", "at": "2026-11-27T21:12:00Z",
      "text": "Can we see Lauderdale Ave this Saturday?" },
    { "id": "note-42", "type": "note", "text": "Two kids starting school in February." }
  ]
}

The reply tells you what was stored and what Tali learned. Leave client_id out to teach Tali about the business itself, such as prices, opening hours and policies.

Voice notes, documents and images

Add media to an event. Tali turns it into text first, then learns from it. These count as answers: voice notes 1 a minute, documents and images 2 each.

{ "business_id": "biz-1", "client_id": "client-1",
  "type": "voice_note",
  "media": { "kind": "audio", "media_type": "audio/mp4", "data": "BASE64..." } }

kind is audio, document (PDF) or image (png, jpeg, webp, gif). Up to 8 MB each.

Answers

POST/v1/answers

Needs business_id and kind. Send client_id for anything about a client.

kindWhat you getAlso send
draftA reply to the client, ready to sendmessage, or Tali uses the last event from the client. channel if you like (email, sms).
briefA short briefing before a call or meeting
summaryWhere things stand with the client
bookingThe best time, picked from the ones you say are freefree_slots: a list of open times, written however you show them
askAn answer to a staff questionquestion

Any kind can take context (text or JSON from your software that Tali should use, such as the listing or the invoice in front of the user), from_name (the staff member), and free_slots. When you send free_slots the reply has a booking with the slot she picked, a title and why.

In the reply, used lists the facts Tali relied on, and noticed lists new facts she spotted. Noticed facts are saved as suggested until a person keeps them. On Platform, actions can name one of your own actions for the user to approve.

POST/v1/answers/{id}/outcome

{ "outcome": "approved" | "edited" | "rejected" }

Memories

GET/v1/memories?business_id=&client_id=&q=

What Tali knows. With client_id you get that client's facts. Without it you get the business's own facts, or everything with scope=all. q searches by words.

POST/v1/memories

{ "business_id", "client_id", "fact", "kind", "until" }. Tell Tali a fact directly. until is a date (YYYY-MM-DD) after which the fact is no longer used.

PATCH/v1/memories/{id}

{ "status": "kept" } keeps something Tali noticed. You can also change fact or until.

DELETE/v1/memories/{id}

Businesses and clients

GET/v1/businesses
PUT/v1/businesses/{business_id}

{ "name" }. Makes or renames a business. You do not have to call this: a brain is made the first time you send its ID anywhere.

GET/v1/businesses/{business_id}/export

Everything in that brain: clients, memories and events.

DELETE/v1/businesses/{business_id}

Deletes the brain and everything in it. Use this when a customer leaves you.

PUT/v1/businesses/{business_id}/clients/{client_id}
DELETE/v1/businesses/{business_id}/clients/{client_id}

Forgets one client: their facts and events. Use this when a person asks to be forgotten.

GET/v1/me

Your plan, mode and answers used this period.

The drop-in panel

The panel is a ready-made Tali for a client's page in your product: what she knows, things she noticed, and buttons to draft a reply, brief the user or suggest a time. It carries your name for her and your colour.

1. Your server makes a short lived token

curl https://talihq.com.au/brain/api/v1/panel-tokens \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer thb_test_..." -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "business_id": "biz-1", "client_id": "client-1", "user_name": "Sam", "minutes": 60 }'

You get back url. The token only works for that one client, and runs out after minutes (5 to 720).

2. Your page shows it

<iframe id="tali" src="URL_FROM_STEP_1" style="width:380px;height:640px;border:0"></iframe>
<script>
  const frame = document.getElementById('tali');
  window.addEventListener('message', (e) => {
    if (e.source !== frame.contentWindow) return;
    if (e.data.type === 'tali:ready') frame.contentWindow.postMessage({
      type: 'tali:context',
      message: 'The message your user is replying to',
      free_slots: ['Sat 28 Nov 10:30am', 'Sat 28 Nov 2:00pm'],
      context: 'Anything on screen Tali should know'
    }, '*');
    if (e.data.type === 'tali:send') putInMessageBox(e.data.text);      // your user still presses send
    if (e.data.type === 'tali:book') openBooking(e.data.slot, e.data.title);
  });
</script>

The panel reports outcomes for you.

Limits and errors

  • Sandbox. 5 businesses and 300 answers a month, free.
  • Your cap. On a plan, answers past the included ones are billed per 1,000 after the period ends. Tali stops at your cap, which starts at three times your included answers. Change it in the console.
  • Speed. 120 requests a minute.
  • Remembering is free within fair use: learning from events up to ten times your plan's answers each period. Past that, events are still stored.

Errors come back as { "error": { "code", "message" } }.

StatuscodeMeaning
400variousSomething is missing or not right. The message says what.
401bad_key, bad_token, expiredThe key or panel token is not valid.
402not_active, business_limitThe licence is not active, or the plan's businesses are all used.
403switched_offThat kind of answer is switched off under Customise.
429slow_down, sandbox_limit, cap_reachedToo fast, or a limit was reached.
502try_againTali could not answer just now. Nothing was charged.

Privacy rules

  • Each business's brain is separate. A key or token can never read another licensee's data, and an answer for one business never uses another business's facts.
  • Tali never keeps card, bank account or government ID numbers, or passwords.
  • She keeps a health, money or legal detail only if the client said it or a staff member wrote it down.
  • Brains are stored in Sydney, Australia. To write an answer, the facts needed are sent to our AI providers, who do not train on them.
  • You can export or delete a brain, or forget one client, at any time. When a licence ends we delete its brains within 30 days.
