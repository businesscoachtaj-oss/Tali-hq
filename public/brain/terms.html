1. What you get

A licence to use Tali Brain inside your own software, for the businesses that use your software: the API, the drop-in panel, the console and the docs. The sandbox is free. Live use needs a plan.

2. What you can and cannot do

  • You can put Tali inside your own product, under the name your plan allows, and charge your customers for it however you like.

3. A person says yes

Tali writes drafts and suggestions. She does not send messages, make bookings or spend money. Your software must only act on an answer after one of your users has approved it. Answers are written by AI and can be wrong, so your users need to read them.

4. Plans, credits, billing and your cap

  • Prices are in Australian dollars. GST is added for Australian companies.
  • Pay as you go: answer credits are paid for up front and cover up to 10 businesses. Each answer uses its credits, and remembering uses 1 credit for every 5 learning calls. Credits can be used for 12 months from your last top up, then any left run out. Credits are not refundable, except where the law requires it.
  • Monthly plans are billed monthly in advance.
  • Each plan includes a number of answers each billing period. Answers past that are billed per 1,000 at your plan's rate after the period ends.
  • You set a cap in the console. Tali stops answering at the cap until you raise it or the next period starts.
  • Pilot has a 3 month minimum. Growth and Platform have a 12 month minimum. Inside a minimum you cannot cancel or move to a cheaper plan from the console. If we agree to end a plan inside its minimum, the remaining months are still payable.
  • On a monthly plan, any credits you have left are used before extra answers are billed.
  • We can change prices with 30 days' notice. A change does not apply inside a minimum term you are already in.
  • If a payment fails and is not fixed within 14 days we can pause your live keys.

5. Your data

  • Each business's brain is kept separate. We never use one licensee's data for another, and we do not use your data to train AI models.
  • Brains are stored in Sydney, Australia. To write an answer, the facts needed are sent to our AI providers (Anthropic and OpenAI), who process them outside Australia and do not train on them.
  • You can export or delete a brain, or forget one client, at any time through the API or the console.
  • When your licence ends we delete your live brains within 30 days.
