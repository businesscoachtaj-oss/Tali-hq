Questions

Good to know

Do my customers deal with you?

No. Tali lives inside your product under the name you choose. Your customers deal with you. You pay us the licence, and what you charge your customers for it is up to you.

Can we pass the licence on to other companies?

No. The licence covers Tali inside your own software, for the businesses on your platform.

Can we start without a monthly plan?

Yes. Pay as you go has no monthly fee. Buy answer credits when you need them, for up to 10 businesses. Credits last 12 months from your last top up. When you outgrow it, move to a plan and any credits left are used before extra answers are billed.

What happens if we go over our answers?

On a plan, extra answers are billed per 1,000 at your plan's rate after the month ends. You set a cap in the console, and Tali stops at the cap until you raise it. On Pay as you go, Tali stops when your credits run out, until you top up.

Where is the data?

Each brain is stored in Sydney, Australia. To write an answer, the facts needed for that answer are sent to our AI providers, who do not use them to train their models. You can export or delete a brain at any time. More on the security page.

Can Tali send messages or make bookings herself?

No. She writes drafts and suggestions. Your software sends or books only after your user says yes.
